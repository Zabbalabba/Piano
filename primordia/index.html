How to play

Swim and eat

WASD or arrow keys to swim, or hold the mouse button to swim toward the pointer. Shift sprints at a high energy cost. On a touchscreen, hold a finger where you want to go and use the Sprint button.

Mouths only work on the side they face. Filter mouths eat algae, bacteria and detritus; jaws bite cells and eat meat.

Grow and divide

Surplus energy fills the Division bar. When it is full, press R to divide and open the editor. Spend DNA on new parts, and pick one free mutation shaped by what this generation went through.

Each division also adds a sibling to your lineage. If you die, you continue as your nearest relative. If the whole lineage dies, it’s over.

Read the water

Every spot has a temperature, light, UV, oxygen and sulfur level. The gauges on the left show where you stand against your tolerances. Going past a limit hurts.

Eras change the whole sea every few minutes, and the forecast tells you what’s coming. The AI species evolve under the same pressure.

Gain new parts

Without eyes, other cells are blurry glows beyond a short distance. An eyespot shows shapes, an eye cup tells threats from prey, an ocelloid identifies species and spots hunters off-screen. Eyes see farthest where they face. Living a generation with an eye lets it evolve to the next tier.

You start with only a few parts unlocked. Others come from mutations, or from horizontal gene transfer: eating cells or their remains can give you their genes.

Tab opens the ecosystem view, P pauses, and the mouse wheel zooms. Progress saves automatically; pick it up again with Continue on the title screen.
